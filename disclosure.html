Affiliate Disclosure

The straightforward version of how ShweetMods makes money and what that means for you.

Who We Are

ShweetMods is an independent controller enthusiast site publishing guides, reviews, and recommendations related to gaming controllers, mods, and accessories. The site is operated by ShweetTea and is not affiliated with any controller manufacturer, retailer, or gaming brand unless explicitly stated.

Amazon Associates

ShweetMods is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

This means that when you click a product link on this site that leads to Amazon and make a qualifying purchase, ShweetMods may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or navigate to Amazon directly.

Other Affiliate Programs

ShweetMods may also participate in affiliate programs with other companies including but not limited to controller manufacturers, accessory brands, and gaming retailers. Any such relationship will be disclosed in the relevant article or page. All affiliate partnerships are subject to the same editorial standards as Amazon — we only link to products we would genuinely recommend regardless of commission.

Editorial Independence

Affiliate relationships do not influence which products we recommend or what we write about them. We do not accept payment to feature, review, or positively mention any product. If a product is recommended on ShweetMods, it is because we believe it genuinely serves the reader, not because we earn more money from it. Downsides are called out as clearly as upsides.

FTC Compliance

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255), ShweetMods discloses any material connection between this site and the products or services mentioned, including affiliate compensation. This disclosure appears on the gear page, at the bottom of relevant articles, and on this page.

Questions

If you have any questions about our affiliate relationships or how we select products, you can submit them on the requests page or check the ShweetMods FAQ for more context on how recommendations are made.
